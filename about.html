About Warung123

Warung123 is a cozy and affordable eatery located in the heart of the city. We offer a variety of delicious local dishes made from fresh ingredients. Our mission is to provide a comfortable dining experience for everyone.

Our history

Established in 2010, Warung123 has been serving the community for over a decade. Founded by a group of food enthusiasts, our warung has grown from a small stall to a popular dining spot. We take pride in our rich history and commitment to quality.
